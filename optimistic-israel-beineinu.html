What Beineinu Is

A year-long move from relationship to co-creation.

Most Israel–North America programs stop at connection: a mission trip, a mixer, a cohort that ends when the relationships do. Beineinu is built to go further. Twenty-four to thirty Israeli and North American leaders working in the same sector spend a year together, moving from genuine relationship into real, working collaboration: prototypes, products, and programs built jointly, not just conversation.

How It Works

Four phases, twelve months.

01

Connect

The cohort comes together for the first time: shared context, shared language, and the relationships every later phase depends on.

02

Immerse

Delegations travel between Israel and North America, spending real time inside each other's professional and cultural worlds, not just meeting rooms.

03

Create

Cross-border pairs and small teams build a working prototype in their shared field: a product, program, or partnership grounded in what they've learned together.

04

Act

Cohort members carry their prototypes home, backed by a documented framework and an advisory council, so the work outlives the program.

Who It's For

Built for leaders ready to build together.

Beineinu is for Israeli and North American leaders in the same sector who are ready to move past connection into collaboration.

  • You lead or influence decisions in your organization or field.
  • You're curious about Israeli–North American partnership, not just supportive of it in theory.
  • You can commit to gatherings and travel across the twelve-month cohort.
  • You want to build something real with your cohort, not just expand your network.

Ready to build something real together?

Beineinu cohorts are small by design. If you're curious whether this is the right fit for you or your organization, let's talk.
