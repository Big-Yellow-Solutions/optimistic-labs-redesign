Who we work with

Built for institutions that know incremental isn't enough.

Most colleges and universities don't have an alumni problem. They have a belonging problem. The mission is sound. The alumni office is working hard. But somewhere between commencement and the next capital campaign, the relationship goes quiet. Alumni Lab works with institutions of every size and stage, from small independent schools building their first alumni program to major public research universities rethinking advancement at scale. We're a good fit for leaders who are ready to question long-held assumptions about what alumni engagement is for, and who want to build something sturdier than the next fundraising push.

What We Do

Three ways we can work with you.

01

Institutional Culture & Belonging Audit

A comprehensive assessment of your institution's culture, alumni and donor experience, organizational alignment, and advancement ecosystem. Through interviews, observation, data analysis, and executive workshops, we identify what's building trust and belonging, what's getting in the way, and deliver a practical roadmap your team can act on.

02

Culture of Hospitality Executive Coaching

An executive coaching and organizational development program for leadership teams, built on a simple premise: your staff can't extend a genuine, compelling invitation to students, alumni, and donors if they don't experience belonging and purpose themselves. We combine leadership coaching, team facilitation, and culture-building practice to make hospitality an institutional posture, not a customer-service script.

03

Impact-Centered Advancement Strategy

A strategic engagement that rethinks annual giving, alumni engagement, and philanthropy through the lens of mission and long-term relationship rather than transactional fundraising. Delivered through executive workshops, planning sessions, and implementation support, drawing on strategy built and tested across institutions from emerging independent schools to large public universities.

How We're Different

Principles we work by.

These beliefs shape every Alumni Lab engagement.

01

We believe belonging comes before giving

Philanthropy isn't the starting point, it's the outcome. When alumni feel genuine belonging and trust, engagement and giving follow. Chase the gift first and you get transactions. Build the relationship first and you get partners for life.

02

We work across the whole lifecycle, not just the alumni office

Belonging isn't built in a single department. It's shaped from a student's first interaction with the institution through decades of alumni involvement. We help connect admissions, student affairs, academic affairs, alumni relations, and advancement into one coherent experience.

03

We bring research and practice together

Our frameworks are grounded in real institutional strategy, tested from small independent schools to major research universities, and sharpened by ongoing research into what actually builds institutional trust. This isn't theory handed down. It's practice built up.

04

We know higher ed from the inside

We've sat inside these institutions, not just advised them from outside. We understand the budget cycles, the politics, the culture, and the stakes, and we meet your leadership team where they actually are.

Let's start with a conversation.

Every engagement begins with a call: no agenda, no pitch. Just a chance to understand where your institution is and whether Alumni Lab is the right fit.
